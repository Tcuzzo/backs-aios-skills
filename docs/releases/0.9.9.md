# BACKS AIOS 0.9.9

## The retired command leaves the shipped pack

`turn-and-burn` no longer answers the retired command. The command name, the
trigger-word spelling of it and the whole family of gap-answer phrasings are out
of the description, the trigger list and the body of every locale mirror, so a
habit that typed the old command now routes to the skill that owns the work.

## The evidence contract has a live owner

The work the retired command used to own now belongs to `truth-audit`. That skill
carries the six rules of the evidence contract as the section it owns, so a reader
who asks what is really running, and what is really broken, gets an answer backed
by evidence instead of an opinion. The `turn-and-burn` body points at it.

## The retired protocol document leaves the pack

The document that shipped beside the retired command,
`skills/turn-and-burn/references/blindspot_protocol.md`, is no longer part of the
pack. Its bytes are preserved byte for byte in the platform's own archive and in
this repository's history, at the hash they were recorded under. The pack ships
the short form of the contract and names the skill that owns the full one.

Nothing is deleted. The bytes survive; only the retired command leaves the
surface.
